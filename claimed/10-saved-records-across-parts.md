# saved-records-across-parts — records and carry through a whole campaign

Walk a mission of several parts through the App from the mission list to results, saving a codename: each part keeps the saved table, bonus word and game-complete flag, a failed part replayed from the fail menu keeps them too, and quitting from the fail menu leaves the saved file unchanged. Check the failed path against the game [M]. Fix anything that drops a record and pin it.
