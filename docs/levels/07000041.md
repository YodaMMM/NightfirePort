space-station approach sequence), not a playable mission. **Completable: n/a (wrapper)** — the
wrapper's own end is the task's known open item (§6).

## 5. The wrapper's end — the task's known open item

`07000041.bin` carries **no exit**: no 0xE8 LOAD_LEVEL placement, no ch-100 writer in the 7
scripts [D the scans]. The retail flow reaches the station **without** the wrapper's data: the
final drive's hand-off resolves to the map (`GetLevelToLoadFromMovieCode(0x7300017) = 0x7000048`
[M 0x1d3560]) and the station is loaded through its own briefing special case —
`ResetMap_LevelToLoad(0x700001b)` upgrades to the 0x7100018 briefing [M 0x1d3800's switch],
`ResetMap_Load` phase 0 masks it back to 0x7000018 and **maps 0x7000018 → 0x700001b** [M
0x1d6360:99-102]. The approach→station story beat is the game's own; the wrapper is its
in-engine authoring ground.

**Port flow [S unverified]** (the task's "it must play through into 0700001b as the game does"):
with the approach chain fired (ch 1 — the scripted-drone plate #99's out [D PL#99]), the wrapper
ends into **0x0700001b** once the chain's longest script (0x06000027, 241 ticks [D the nis dump])
has run — `Drones::tick_inner`'s `wrapper_end_timer` arm (drone.rs), gated `level == 0x07000041`.
The test pins the port's flow (`the_wrapper_ends_into_the_space_station_after_the_chain`, with
the no-chain sister) and is marked [S] — the timer length and the destination are the port's
choice, the retail mechanism is not observable in the disc data.
