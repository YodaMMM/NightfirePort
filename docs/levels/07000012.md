### The shaft-lobby funnel (measured 2026-10-18, probes 12/12b/12c/12d)

The lobby's floor is a RAMP (class 0x0a, y 1.14 @z 9.4 → 0.32 @z 13.4) with a floor trench at x 5.0..5.5 (no
floor to −2). Its entry through the north wall (south face z 8.78 x ≤ 4.8, the notch x 4.9..5.1 recessed to
9.10, the round pilaster class 0x05 spanning x 5.25..6.12 with its south face sloping z 9.00→8.60) pinches to
**0.88 at z ≈ 8.7** between the pilaster and the core's west wall (class 0x15, x 7.00): the r 0.55 capsule
[M Player_Collision 0x194df0] would need x ∈ [6.67, 6.45] = ∅. The gap opens to 1.75 at z 9.0 and 2.65 at z 9.3,
so the game's cylinder move (Intersect_CylGeom 0x109da0) passes it by resolving the penetration along the move
instead of a full two-sided depenetration — the same funnel class as the door[19] pincer. Open live check: watch Bond's xz cross the wall plane (z 9.1..9.3) near x ≈ 5.5–6.5 on a real console and record the
path.
