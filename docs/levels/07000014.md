**The way out (level-14-vent-grate, 2026-10-22 — supersedes level-14-complete's bit-0x1 skip).**
The south duct's grate is the STATIC ART object **"AirConGrill1"** (24 tris baked absolute at
z=58.86, the six 0.06-0.07-thick bar bands y 2.62..3.62 [D probe14_grateowner/probe14_tris]).
Its placement #100 carries **0x00008019** — kind 0x19 with the **0x8000 create-cel bit set** — and
its glist word is **0x1** [D probe14_plcflags]. The game gives it a runtime cel
([M] `parsemap_block_map_data_static` 0x1d1998:92 creates a cel iff the placement flags carry
0x8000; :103 copies the glist word to cel+0x94) and **never** skips it:
[M] `Collide_Pick` 0x1eb320 — the candidate stage of the movement capsule's kind-0x800 query
(`Collide_CylinderIntersect` 0x1ecc00:61) — drops nodes only on cel+0x94 **bit 0x20** (:140) and
**bit 0x1000 with query+0x98 bit 0x20** (:145-151). No bit-0x1 test exists in the pick path, so
**the game's grill blocks and nothing opens it**: the lid SPs' script 0x06000A3B spawns the
fusebox entity 0x02000750 (type 0x31) and the ladder art 0x02000751 (type 25) and animates no
lid [D probe14_lidscript], and the mode-0x40 preload is one tick of exactly that
([M] `SP_LoadScript` 0x1dbc08:46-54 — any mode outside {1, 8} runs Play+Update+Stop, state 1).
96 bit-0x1 cel-placements on this level carry tris the game KEEPS (grills, the fixed
ServerRoomGlass1-5 panes, labels, chairbases [D probe14_plcflags]); the door twins
(Door_Slide_*, kind 0xdb, plc 0x000000db) and the Breakable windows (kind 0x20, plc 0x00000020)
carry **no 0x8000** and never had a static cel at all. The old bit-0x1 skip
(level-14-complete) therefore dropped real collision game-wide and is REVERTED; the port's
`build_scoped` now skips glist **bit 0x20** objects instead ([M] Collide_Pick :140; the
CargoArea*Shadows glist 0x21 [D]) and the crawl leaves through the EAST duct mouth
(the route legs below keep the older west-descent text pending the rewrite — the east lane
z≈65.4-65.5 reaches x≈45.3 past lid SP #328, then descends south to the stairwell slot
approach (45.23, 1.07, 56.27) [D probe14_eastduct]; the mouth lintel stops the port's real-input
crawl at x=40.98 — live check).

Beyond the grill the walked route hits two dead ends [D probe14_stairs/probe14_trench]: the duct
stairs (steps z 57.2..57.8 y 2.40→1.79, a ramp to y≈1.05) end at a shaft slot ~0.4-0.55 deep
(z 55.72..56.1; the south wall face y≈0.4..3.0, normal +z; open DOWN to y=-3.5 — the facility

sleeper drone #596 waits) has no entrance from the duct floor found yet. Live checks in the

  objective reads those channels [D].
