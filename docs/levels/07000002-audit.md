## GAPS

**GAP-1 (large, open on 01 too — the RB props).** 07000002 has 21 kind-0xD8 placements [D]:
`bucketswing` #4, `rb_redlamp` #6 and 19 `rb_greenlamp` garden lamps. The game creates them with
`RB_Create` 0x1d6bf0 (case 0xd8, placement +0x2c mass, +0x34 type halfword) as pushable/shootable
props with wind + Euler integration + bullet impulses [M 0x1d6e38, RB_Init 0x1d7ad8,
RB_CuboidMomentsOfInertia 0x1d7dc8, RB_Build 0x1d7ba0, RB_SetState 0x1d7da0]. The port's
`rigid_body.rs` ports the init math but nothing integrates it: the props are drawn as statics and
do not react to bullets or Bond. Chain: 0x1d04f0 case 0xd8 → `RB_Create` → `RB_SetState` live →
`RB_Update` wind/Euler (0x1d6e38) → `Collide_*` bullet impulses. This is the same disposition 01
shipped with (07000001.md: "⚠ still draw-only"); it stays its own task (shared by every level with
0xD8 placements).

**GAP-2 (small — fixed by this task).** `07000002.md`'s Destroyables line reads "cable lights
#390/#392 (smash → ch 0x82, kills the electric arc)". The disc gives **#390 out channel 0** and
**#392 out channel 0x82** [D placements param 0]. Only the middle light feeds the arc chain; #390
and #394 are channel-less smash props. Doc corrected; the new census test pins all six out
channels (below).

**GAP-3 (note, not a defect).** Wire #187 + ThirdIcon #384 are created and resolve, but no 02
test drives a full hand-over-hand crossing of the cable gap (the ladder/wire suites cover the
mechanics on their own levels). A drive test would be a nice-to-have, not a behaviour gap.
